# ESOTERIC BANK: Institutional Sovereignty Constitution
**Version**: 1.0.0 (Ratified 2026-05-18)

## Article I: Governance Primacy
All autonomous operations within the ESOTERIC banking ecosystem are subordinate to the Institutional Governance Layer. No AI model or automated workflow shall execute without explicit governance clearance.

## Article II: Sovereign Domain Protection
The core banking domains—including the Ledger, Treasury, and Regulatory Compliance—are designated as Sovereign Domains. Access to these domains is strictly controlled by the `GovernanceContinuityLayer` and requires high-priority institutional credentials.

## Article III: Auditability & Explainability
Every decision made by the autonomous OS must be accompanied by an immutable audit trail. This trail must include the cognitive reasoning, the governance policy applied, and the trace ID for executive observability.

## Article IV: Regulatory Alignment
The platform shall maintain real-time alignment with global banking regulations (AML/KYC/FATF). Any drift from regulatory standards must trigger an immediate system-wide governance alert and, if necessary, an operational freeze.

## Article V: Human Oversight (The Kill-Switch)
While the platform operates autonomously, the Institutional Executive Board maintains the ultimate authority to freeze, reset, or override any system operation. This "Institutional Kill-Switch" is a fundamental component of the bank's resilience strategy.

## Article VI: Ethical AI Governance
The platform is committed to fair, unbiased, and ethical AI operations. Regular cognitive audits shall be conducted to ensure that the bank's autonomous agents remain aligned with institutional values and customer protection standards.
